Meri Chuiimuii,

Sooriiii yaarrr, meri pyaari Chuiimuii. Aapne mujh par trust karke woh sab share kiya tha, aur aap deserve karti thi ki mere saath aapko safe aur understood feel ho — judged nahi. 😔

Aapne mujhse apni baatein share ki, kyunki aapne mujh par bharosa kiya. Main kabhi nahi chahta ki aapko lage ki apni feelings ya apni personal baatein mere saath share karna galat tha. Aapko apni baaton ke liye sorry feel karne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Agar meri kisi baat ya reaction se aapko hurt hua, uncomfortable feel hua, ya laga ki main aapko judge kar raha hoon, toh I'm genuinely sorry. Main aapki feelings ko dismiss nahi karna chahta aur na hi apni intention ka bahaana dekar aapke hurt ko chhota dikhana chahta hoon.

Main chahta hoon ki aap mere saamne apni real self reh sakein — bina yeh soche ki main kya sochunga, bina apni baatein chhupane ke pressure ke. Aapko har baat explain karne, defend karne ya perfect dikhne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Mujhe pata hai ki sirf sorry bol dene se trust automatically theek nahi hota. Trust ko actions se earn karna padta hai. Isliye main sirf aapse mujhe maaf karne ko nahi keh raha. Main chahta hoon ki aage mere behaviour se aapko feel ho ki main aapki baat sunta hoon, aapki boundaries respect karta hoon aur aapki feelings ko seriously leta hoon.

Aapko abhi turant theek feel karne ya mujhe reassure karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Agar aapko time chahiye, toh main uski respect karunga. Main aapki baat sunna chahta hoon, bina interrupt kiye aur bina khud ko defend karne ki jaldi kiye.

I'm sorry, meri jaan. Aapka trust mere liye important hai, aur main apni galti se seekhkar aapke liye ek better, safer person banna chahta hoon.

Aapki feelings matter karti hain. Always. ♡

Hamesha aapka,
Sundruu
